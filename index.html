GlowMirror / 照见你

Privacy Policy

GlowMirror is designed to work like a simple mirror on your phone. That means the app keeps things intentionally narrow: camera access for live mirror preview, no account system, no ads, and no data collection for analytics or tracking.

Information We Collect

GlowMirror does not collect personal information from you.

  • No account creation
  • No profile data
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers

Camera Access

GlowMirror requests access to your iPhone camera only to provide a real-time mirror preview. The camera feed is used inside the app and is not uploaded to our servers.

On-Device Processing

Image processing and display adjustments happen on your device. GlowMirror is built to help you see yourself more clearly on different iPhone models, at a comfortable holding distance, and in dim environments with automatic fill light.

Ads and Tracking

GlowMirror does not show third-party ads and does not use third-party tracking technologies.

In-App Purchases

If you choose to unlock paid features, payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children's Privacy

GlowMirror is not designed to collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's privacy behavior changes in the future, this page will be updated before or when those changes ship.
