Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Updated November 24, 2026

Photos, camera and face tracking

The photo picker gives GlowMirror the images you choose. Reference photos and live previews are processed on your device, not sent to a server or remote AI service. On supported devices, face tracking positions the lip color. Camera frames and facial landmarks are processed in memory; we do not collect or share them. You can turn camera access off in Settings.

What stays on your device

Saved shades, wardrobe references and your current selection are kept in local app storage. Remove saved items in the app, or delete the app to remove its local data. Device backups may contain earlier copies. A limited local diagnostic log records session states and timing, not photos or raw face landmarks. It is shared only if you choose to share it.

Purchases and support

Apple handles purchases and restores through the App Store. GlowMirror checks whether paid features are available; we do not receive your card details. The app has no account, third-party ads or tracking SDK. If you email us, we use the address and information you provide to answer your request. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, use the email above.
